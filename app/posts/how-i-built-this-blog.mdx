# The Story That Started It All

It’s not like I just woke up one day and went, *“Ykw? I think building a blog would be nice…”*

Nope, the story would be boring if it went like that. This all started in the middle of the night, *"Oh boy, I'm bored as heck..."*

And you know how it goes—while normal people would probably call it a night and go to bed, I? I started a brand-new project.  
Yep, right there, in the middle of the night—whilst my other two projects sat in the corner of my screen, waiting for me to *𝓽𝓸𝓾𝓬𝓱* them.

---

# Why Build a Blog?

Welp, I actually do have a nice reason for this.  
Why build a blog, and not something else?

At the time, I had two things that bugged me:

- I needed something to help me track where I went wrong, so I wouldn't fall into the same traps twice.
- I wanted something cool (so I could flex to my friends :D).

So… isn’t a blog the perfect answer to both?  
(well, the first reason is the main one.)

Like a wise owl once told me:

> “You tend to forgor, while ink rember.”  
>
> — a veri wise owl indeed.

# What Did I Use?

I mainly used **Next.js** and **Tailwind CSS**, plus a few other helpful libraries that made things smoother.

If you're new to this kind of thing, don’t worry—I'll break it down so you get the rough idea.
